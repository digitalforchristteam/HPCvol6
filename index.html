Email settings

Automatic email (recommended): create a free Brevo account (no card), verify your sender email, make an API key, then in Cloudflare open your Worker, Settings, Variables and secrets, and add BREVO_KEY (the key) and MAIL_FROM (your verified email). RSVP emails and scheduled reminders then send even when this site is closed. Below is the older browser-based option.

Free setup with EmailJS (200 emails a month, no card reported as of today). Create an account at emailjs.com, connect your email, make a template that uses {{to_email}}, {{subject}} and {{message}}, then paste the three codes here. A backup copy uses one extra email.

Free ways to use WhatsApp

Most efficient: one WhatsApp group per ministry or event. Tap Share on an event to post it, add a WhatsApp poll for Yes or No, and message only the people who have not replied.

Also free: one-to-one WhatsApp buttons, Communities and Channels. Meta's automatic business service now charges per message, so we do not use it.

Your data and privacy

This is the front-end first release. Data is stored in your own browser, so it is not yet shared between people. Accounts, shared database, roles and email reminders come next, using Cloudflare’s free tools.
